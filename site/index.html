Greeble

Greeble dresses your blocked-out zones with your own assets, in your studio's style.

Install the Studio plugin

Block it out. Greeble dresses it.

Greeble is a Roblox Studio plugin that places props and detail on a blocked-out map, using your studio's own asset kit.

  • Learn style. Select a finished map and Greeble reads how your team dresses a place.
  • Dress. Select a blocked-out zone and Greeble applies that style with your own assets. It leaves steep slopes alone.
  • Edit. What it places are normal Studio instances. Move them, delete them, or undo the whole pass.

Bring your own agent

The Greeble pack plugs into the coding agent you already use, such as Claude Code. Your agent drives Studio; the plugin listens and does the placing.

Ask for a zone to be dressed in the same breath as the rest of your work. Greeble never generates meshes: every piece comes from your asset kit.

Or just ask, inside Studio

No agent of your own? Open the chat panel in Studio and talk to our hosted agent. There is nothing to set up.

Your map data goes to our server, never through a model.
