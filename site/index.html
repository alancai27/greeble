Greeble

Greeble dresses your blocked-out zones with your own assets, in your studio's style.

Install the Studio plugin

Block it out. Greeble dresses it.

Rough in a zone with plain parts, select it and press Dress. Greeble fills it with trees, rocks, fences and props from your own asset library, placed the way your team would place them.

Nothing is generated from scratch. Every piece is something you already own, so the map looks like your game from the first pass.

Your pack, your style, your workflow

Point Greeble at a finished map and it learns your style: what goes near paths, how dense the edges get, which props cluster together. Point it at a folder of models and that becomes the pack it draws from.

Dress a zone, keep what works, undo what doesn't. It all happens inside Studio, with the usual history and no export step.

The heavy lifting runs on our side

Style learning and placement run on hosted tools, so the plugin stays light and your place file stays yours. Your team shares one style profile, kept on the server and ready on every machine.

Free to try, simple to keep

Install the plugin and dress your first maps at no cost. Plans for studios that dress every day are on the way.
